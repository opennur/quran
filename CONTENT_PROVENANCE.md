# Content Provenance

The bundled Arabic text and Indonesian translation are fetched from the
[equran.id API](https://equran.id/apidev). The app uses the fields `teksArab`
and `teksIndonesia` from each surah response and preserves the source text
without applying transliteration or tajwid transformations.

The Arabic font is Amiri from Google Fonts and is distributed under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1.

Before public distribution, confirm the current redistribution and attribution
terms for the Quran text and Indonesian translation with their providers.
